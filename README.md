## 🚀 Experience & Impact

### 🏟️ Data Engineering & Analytics @ Hookflash
*Jan 2025 – Present*

I act as the technical bridge for clients across **Sport, Retail, and Healthcare**, translating raw data into strategic assets.
*   **Cloud Integration:** Architected a unified cloud environment for a top-tier football club by integrating **nine disparate data platforms**. This eliminated data silos and provided leadership with real-time visibility into engagement and commercial performance.
*   **High-Scale Retail:** Managing the data analytics function for numerous retail clients with millions of monthly users, ensuring data integrity and actionable insights are available to drive marketing investment.
*   **Predictive Modelling:** I developed forecasting models for a major charity which allowed them to plan budgets and resource allocation with greater certainty.

### 🛡️ Strategic Consulting @ Ministry of Defence
*Sept 2023 – Dec 2023*

Operating within the "Mission Driven Entrepreneurship" framework, I led a consultancy team for the **Defence School of Intelligence**.
*   **Problem Discovery:** We conducted deep-dive interviews with MoD personnel, civil servants, and contractors to cut through the noise and identify the root causes of organisational friction.
*   **Solution Design:** We moved beyond theory to deliver a practical implementation roadmap that balanced strict security requirements with the need for organisational agility.

### 🏭 Business Systems Analysis @ Manufacturing Sector
*Jan 2023 – June 2023*

Centred on operational efficiency, I worked directly with leadership to map the "as-is" operating model and identify critical bottlenecks.
*   **Operating Model Analysis:** I collaborated with an Associate Director to produce a comprehensive report on the operating model, highlighting organisational bottlenecks through rigorous data synthesis and interview analysis.
*   **Workflow Optimisation:** I applied process mapping and SWOT analysis to identify specific delays in procurement approvals.
*   **Evidence-Based Change:** My final report and presentation to the board directly influenced changes to improve turnaround times.
